About

I run creator campaigns and programmes.

I set measurement up from the start, and brief creators with both creative freedom and clear strategic direction.

That means the content feels natural, recognisable and engaging to their audiences, while still working towards clear brand goals.

Most brands are already working with creators. Fewer can say what the impact actually is. The relationships live in somebody's DMs, and the reporting arrives too late to change anything.

I take it off their hands. A single campaign, or an ongoing roster that gets better the longer it runs.

I choose creators who genuinely fit rather than the ones with the biggest following. I came up through brand and campaigns rather than only influencer marketing, so I can take a campaign platform and turn it into a brief that gives them room to make it their own without losing the point.

Most of my work has been in health. Breast cancer awareness, postpartum recovery, sexual health, and audiences who are usually talked past rather than talked to. The instinct in those areas is to control the language tightly. I keep the accuracy and I keep the creator's voice, because the second one is what makes the first one land.

Five years across Elvie, CoppaFeel! and Dojo. Now freelance, working with UK and European brands.

If creators are on your plan for this year or next, I'd like to hear about it.

Creator Programme | Annual Awareness Campaign

Brought CoppaFeel!'s creator programme back in-house from an agency and scaled it into a core channel for the annual campaign.

"The healthiest 'check your chest' promo I've ever seen!"

£531K+EMV from a £100K creator budget

Figures cover the creator programme only. The £100K budget comprises creator fees and the paid amplification behind that content. Earned media value calculated in Sprout Influencer Manager.

Context

This campaign is one of the biggest moments of the whole year for the charity. It has to get 18 to 24 year olds checking their chests, which is a hard ask on platforms built for entertainment.

The creator programme had previously been run externally.

Role

Led the creator programme end to end: strategy, sourcing, briefing, relationship management, paid amplification and reporting.

Approach

I brought it in-house myself: finding creators, having the conversations, and holding the relationships afterwards so they belonged to the charity rather than to an agency.

The brief was the part I cared about most. With a subject like breast cancer, the temptation is to hand creators a script. We kept the messaging clear and accurate, because that part isn't negotiable with health content. Everything else was theirs: how they told it, what felt authentic to their audience.

Seven creators, all aligned to 18 to 24 audiences. TikTok and Instagram, with paid amplification behind the work.

Result

Creators are now a core channel for the campaign, and CoppaFeel! owns the relationships.

UGC Programme | Women's Health Tech

Built a UGC programme from nothing to fuel organic social, paid social and product launches.

6.5MViews (vs. 2.2M target)
3×View target exceeded
10+Creators

Context

We were paying expensive influencers for one or two videos every month or two. Good content, but nowhere near the volume of short-form video we needed to feed paid and organic.

Role

Built the creator network from nothing, and ran it over one to two years.

Approach

I built a network of early-stage creators rather than a handful of expensive names, and invested properly in getting them good: on the product, and on how to actually make content that performs.

That meant kitting some of them out with basics like ring lights, and running monthly and quarterly reviews where we looked at what was and wasn't working and pushed the content further.

Once they understood the product and the format, we could produce at a volume and pace a handful of high-fee partnerships never could, and reactively enough to move with what was happening on the platforms.

Result

TargetDelivered
2.2M views6.5M views

2.2M views was the target. We finished at 6.5M, from over 100 videos and a roster of ten-plus creators the brand owned outright rather than rented from an agency.

The point was never the view count. It was that Elvie ended up with a content engine running at the speed the platforms actually move at.

Context

Research showed lower awareness and self-checking among young people with South Asian heritage. Part of the reason was that existing health messaging and materials lacked culturally relevant representation.

Role

Owned strategy, creator management and paid social end to end.

Approach

We ran focus groups before writing anything.

That shaped a three part series moving from awareness through to actually checking, rather than one post hoping to do everything at once. We worked with creators from the community, and put paid behind it on TikTok and Meta.

Result

Context

Postpartum sex is something an enormous number of people are quietly worried about and almost nobody discusses publicly. For a brand, it is the kind of subject where the safe move is to say nothing at all.

Role

Owned strategy, research, execution and reporting end to end for Sexual Health Month, a new content pillar established to cover postpartum sexual wellbeing.

Approach

It started in social listening. The questions people were asking were more specific, and more emotionally loaded, than anything we were publishing could address.

I ran academic and qualitative research to define what the audience was actually struggling with, and built the campaign around that. Hannah Witton carried it on YouTube and Instagram, with creator content from the UGC programme underneath, plus email and editorial.

Brand & Design Governance | Organisation-Wide Creative Operations

Owned brand guardianship and design operations across the whole organisation, turning an ad hoc request process into a structured, scalable system.

150+Creative briefs/year
+6ptsLogo awareness YoY
Org-wideBrand & design function owned

Context

Following a brand refresh, design requests from across the organisation lacked a clear process, creating inconsistency, bottlenecks and inefficient use of a small design team’s time.

Role

Sole point of contact for all organisation-wide design briefs. Owned the design process end-to-end and managed a freelance design team.

Approach

  • Diagnosed inconsistencies and bottlenecks in the existing briefing process
  • Designed and rolled out a new structured intake process (via Asana), with training for the wider organisation
  • Embedded brand refresh learnings into updated guidelines (secondary colours, iconography, etc.)
  • Reviewed and fed back on 150+ design briefs and assets a year, across marketing, fundraising, education and partnerships teams
  • Onboarded and managed a freelance design team, including coaching and performance support
  • Set clear, fair boundaries on feedback and turnaround to protect quality without slowing delivery

Why this matters

Turned a reactive design function into a structured, scalable process, delivering 150+ briefs a year within budget, while brand recognition rose 6 percentage points in the year following the rebrand.
